# Infrastructure Scour Deficiency History Dashboard

## Overview

A historical trend dashboard developed to monitor documentation deficiencies over time and measure progress toward improving infrastructure data quality.

---

## Business Problem

Operational teams could identify current deficiencies, but they lacked visibility into whether documentation quality was improving or deteriorating over time.

Without historical reporting, it was difficult to:

- Measure district performance
- Evaluate cleanup initiatives
- Identify recurring issues
- Communicate long-term progress to leadership

---

## Solution

Designed a historical Tableau dashboard that tracks monthly deficiency counts across districts, enabling users to analyze trends and monitor improvements over multiple reporting periods.

The dashboard provides a longitudinal view of documentation quality while highlighting districts requiring additional attention.

---

## Key Features

- Monthly historical trend reporting
- District-by-district comparisons
- Heatmap visualization
- Grand total summaries
- Trend monitoring
- Historical deficiency tracking
- Interactive filtering

---

## Technologies

- Tableau Desktop
- Tableau Prep
- SQL
- Historical Data Modeling
- Data Warehousing Concepts

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Time-Series Analysis
- Historical Reporting
- Data Modeling
- KPI Development
- Trend Analysis
- Dashboard Design
- Business Intelligence

---

## Business Impact

Enabled stakeholders to move beyond simply identifying deficiencies by providing visibility into long-term performance trends.
